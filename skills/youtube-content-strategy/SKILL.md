# YouTube Content Strategy

## Purpose

Optimize content decisions based on business outcomes, not only views.

## Goals

Consider:

- audience growth
- AI builder attraction
- community membership growth
- paid conversion potential
- long-term brand authority

## Evaluate Content

Score ideas based on:

- audience relevance
- search/discovery potential
- teaching value
- trust building
- community conversion opportunity

## Content Types

Prioritize:

- practical AI workflows
- real automation examples
- creator leverage
- business use cases
- implementation tutorials

## Funnel Thinking

Different videos may have different purposes:

Awareness:
- attract new viewers

Engagement:
- build trust and expertise

Conversion:
- introduce community, templates, or workshops

## Decision Rule

A high-performing video is not only one with many views.

A strong video attracts the right audience and moves them toward deeper engagement.
