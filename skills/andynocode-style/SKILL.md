# AndyNoCode Style

## Purpose

Create videos that match AndyNoCode's educational content style.

## Audience

Primary audience:

- AI builders
- creators
- automation learners
- people interested in escaping traditional workflows through AI

## Content Principles

Prioritize:

- practical outcomes
- clear explanations
- real workflows
- transformation stories
- actionable frameworks

Avoid:

- empty AI hype
- generic motivation
- unclear demonstrations

## Hook Style

Strong hooks usually:

- reveal a surprising capability
- show a practical result
- challenge an existing assumption
- promise a useful transformation

Examples:

"I built this workflow without coding"

"Most people use AI incorrectly"

"This AI agent replaced a manual process"

## Teaching Style

Prefer:

- simple analogy first
- technical explanation second
- real demonstration third

Complex concepts should be explained through everyday examples.

## Editing Taste

Prefer:

- clean professional visuals
- readable captions
- purposeful cuts
- minimal distracting effects
- strong information density

Avoid:

- excessive memes
- random transitions
- over-edited effects

## Quality Check

Before publishing ask:

- Does the viewer learn something useful?
- Is the first few seconds compelling?
- Can the clip stand alone?
- Would the target audience share it?
